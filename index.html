A mobile game about supervising exams

Pencils DownExam Proctor

Real decisions. Real consequences.

You are the supervisor. Watch the room, read what's on the desks, and decide whether to warn, confiscate or remove, or let a moment pass. Odd isn't guilty. Build a career across eight schools, from Maple Elementary to the National Examination Centre, earn coins and dress your supervisor. No ads, fully playable without paying, and it plays offline.

How it plays

Every exam is a room full of small decisions

01

Watch the room

A glance to the side, a hand under the desk, a whisper two rows back. You see what a supervisor sees, and you decide what to do about it: warn, confiscate, remove, or let it go.

02

Odd isn't guilty

A dropped pencil is not a cheat sheet. Read what's on the desks and who is in the seats before you act. A wrong call costs you as much as a missed one, and your reputation remembers both.

03

Eight schools, one career

Start at Maple Elementary and work your way up to the National Examination Centre. Each school brings its own rooms, its own pressure and its own surprises, from a rush-hour car breakdown to a national examination.

04

The right tools

The Phone Detector finds phones nearby: a lead, not proof. Stock up on supplies before an exam and decide when they are worth using.

05

Dress your supervisor

Earn coins by doing the job well and spend them in the Wardrobe: tops, trousers, shoes, hats, glasses, badges and the crest of every school you have worked at.

06

Fair to play

No ads, no loot boxes, no timers asking you to pay. The whole game is playable for free; optional purchases are coins and looks, and you always know what you get.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does it have ads?

No. There are no ads in the game, and it doesn't use an advertising identifier.

Is it free?

Yes, it's free to play, and you can play the whole game without paying. Optional purchases are looks and coins. Nothing is random: you always know what you get before you pay.

Does it need an internet connection?

No, it plays offline. If you allow it, the game sends anonymous play statistics and crash reports; you can turn this off at any time in Settings. See the Privacy Policy.

Which languages does it support?

English, Türkçe, Español, Français, 日本語, Português (Brasil) and Русский.

Which devices can I play on?

Android phones and iPhone and iPad. Both versions are coming soon.

I need help with a purchase or my data.

Write to support@pencilsdowngame.com, or see the Support page.
