The short version

  • No account, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and no sale or sharing of your data.
  • With your permission (or, outside the regions listed below, until you turn it off), the game sends usage statistics and crash reports, tied to a random app identifier (not to your name or an account) to Google Firebase. These help us fix bugs and balance the game.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • Your career, coins and settings are stored on your device.

What the game stores on your device

The game keeps these files in its private app storage:

  • your career (progress, coins, items, the current career's choices) and a backup of it;
  • your preferences (sound, text size, theme, language, and your analytics choice);
  • your purchase record (what this device has bought, so a career reset never costs you a purchase, and coins you bought survive a reset).

No other app can read them. Uninstalling the game deletes them. "Abandon career" deletes the career, but not your purchases.

On Android, your device's own backup (Google's Auto Backup, if you have it on) may copy these files to your Google account, so they can come back on a new phone. You control this in your phone's settings.

The game uses Google Firebase, provided by Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited in the EEA, UK and Switzerland). Google acts as our data processor under the Google Analytics and Firebase data processing terms.

What is never sent:

  • your name or email;
  • the generated names of people in the game;
  • anything you type;
  • your contacts, photos or files;
  • precise location;
  • an advertising identifier. The game asks for no advertising ID and has no ads.

We do not use the data to identify you, to build advertising profiles, or for any purpose other than those in the table. Firebase may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under Google's standard contractual clauses. For players in Türkiye, see the KVKK section below.

Your choice (consent and opt-out)

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Türkiye, nothing in the table above is collected until you agree on the privacy card the game shows at first launch. The same applies if the game cannot tell which country you are in, and on iOS at present.
  • Everywhere else, collection is on until you turn it off.
  • You can change your answer at any time: Settings → Privacy and terms → "Usage and crash data". Turning it off stops all collection at once and clears the analytics data stored on your device.
  • Data already sent: ask us at support@pencilsdowngame.com to delete it. We will ask Google to delete it.

Purchases

The game sells optional coin packs and item packs. Item packs hold appearance items; a country pack also holds a set of exam names. Buying them sends us no new data about you.

  • Payment is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies.
  • We receive only a confirmation (the product and a transaction identifier), which we use to give you what you bought and to restore it.
  • We do not receive your card details, name or billing address.
  • Coins you buy are stored on your device and survive a career reset. Item packs can be restored with "Restore purchases"; coins cannot.

Children

The game is rated for ages 13 and up. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the game with collection on, contact us and we will delete the data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you;
  • correct or delete it;
  • object to or restrict its use;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • receive it in a portable form;
  • complain to a data protection authority.

Because we hold no account, we identify your data by the identifier on your device. Contact us and we will tell you how to find it.

Türkiye (KVKK, Law No. 6698):

  • The Turkish version of this policy, https://pencilsdowngame.com/tr/privacy/, is our information notice (aydınlatma metni) under Article 10 of the KVKK. The data controller is Arda Kabadayı, the developer named at the top of this policy.
  • Legal basis: analytics, crash reports and Remote Config only with your explicit consent (Art. 5/1), given on the privacy card. The purchase record that stays on your device is needed to perform the purchase (Art. 5/2-c).
  • Transfer abroad: Google processes the data on servers outside Türkiye, including in the United States, under Google's data processing terms. For players in Türkiye, nothing is collected until they give explicit consent on the privacy card.
  • Your rights under Art. 11: to learn whether your data is processed and request information about it; to learn the purpose and whether it is used for that purpose; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad it is transferred to; to have it corrected, deleted or destroyed, and those third parties told; to object to a result against you from purely automated analysis (the game does none); and to claim compensation for unlawful processing.
  • How to apply: email support@pencilsdowngame.com. We answer free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If we refuse, our answer is insufficient, or we don't answer in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu) within 30 days of our answer, and in any case within 60 days of your application.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy and terms.

United States (California and other state privacy laws):

  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • The categories collected are listed in the table above: identifiers (random app identifiers), internet or other electronic activity (game events), and diagnostics.
  • You may request access or deletion at support@pencilsdowngame.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.
  • Collection can be turned off in Settings at any time.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted at https://pencilsdowngame.com/privacy/ with a new effective date. When a change affects what is collected, the game will ask again where consent is required.
