# Privacy Policy: Pencils Down: Exam Proctor

**Effective date:** November 22, 2026  
**Developer (controller):** Arda Kabadayı  
**Contact:** support@pencilsdowngame.com

## The short version

- No account, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and no sale or sharing of your
  data.
- With your permission (or, outside the regions listed below, until you turn it off), the game
  sends **anonymous play statistics and crash reports** to Google Firebase. These help us fix bugs
  and balance the game.
- Purchases are handled entirely by **Google Play** or the **App Store**. We never see your payment
  details.
- Your career, coins and settings are stored **on your device**.

## What the game stores on your device

The game keeps these files in its private app storage:

- your career (progress, coins, items, the current career's choices) and a backup of it;
- your preferences (sound, text size, theme, language, and your analytics choice);
- your purchase record (what this device has bought, so a career reset never costs you a purchase,
  and coins you bought survive a reset).

No other app can read them. Uninstalling the game deletes them. "Abandon career" deletes the
career, but not your purchases.

On Android, your device's own backup (Google's Auto Backup, if you have it on) may copy these
files to your Google account, so they can come back on a new phone. You control this in your
phone's settings.

## What is sent, to whom, and why

The game uses Google Firebase, provided by Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited in the EEA, UK and
Switzerland). **Google acts as our data processor** under the Google Analytics and Firebase data
processing terms.

| Service | What it receives | Why | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Analytics for Firebase** | <ul><li>Game events: exams started and finished, actions taken, tutorial steps, promotions, coins earned and spent (amounts and reasons), store visits and purchases (product and price).</li><li>A random app-instance identifier.</li><li>Device model, operating system, language, app version, an approximate location derived from your IP address (country or region; the IP address itself is not stored).</li><li>Session times.</li></ul> | To understand how the game is played, where players get stuck, and how the economy is balanced. | 14 months, then deleted |
| **Firebase Crashlytics** | <ul><li>Crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, OS version, free memory and disk, app version.</li><li>A short log of the steps before a crash (screen, exam number, school, theme).</li><li>A random installation identifier.</li></ul> | To find and fix crashes. | 90 days |
| **Firebase Remote Config** | A random installation identifier and the app version. | To adjust a few balance values (such as coin pack sizes) without an update. | Only as long as needed to answer the request |

**What is never sent:**

- your name or email;
- the generated names of people in the game;
- anything you type;
- your contacts, photos or files;
- precise location;
- an advertising identifier. The game asks for no advertising ID and has no ads.

We do not use the data to identify you, to build advertising profiles, or for any purpose other
than those in the table. Firebase may process data on servers outside your country, including in
the United States, under Google's standard contractual clauses. For players in Türkiye, see the
KVKK section below.

## Your choice (consent and opt-out)

- **In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Türkiye**, nothing in the
  table above is collected until you agree on the privacy card the game shows at first launch.
  The same applies if the game cannot tell which country you are in, and on iOS at present.
- **Everywhere else**, collection is on until you turn it off.
- **You can change your answer at any time:** Settings → Privacy and terms → "Usage and crash
  data". Turning it off stops all collection at once and clears the analytics data stored on your
  device.
- **Data already sent:** ask us at support@pencilsdowngame.com to delete it. We will ask Google to
  delete it.

## Purchases

The game sells optional coin packs and item packs. Item packs hold appearance items; a country
pack also holds a set of exam names. Buying them sends us no new data about you.

- Payment is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies.
- We receive only a confirmation (the product and a transaction identifier), which we use to give
  you what you bought and to restore it.
- We do not receive your card details, name or billing address.
- Coins you buy are stored on your device and survive a career reset. Item packs can be restored
  with "Restore purchases"; coins cannot.

## Children

The game is rated for ages 13 and up. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not
knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the game with collection on,
contact us and we will delete the data.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

- access the data we hold about you;
- correct or delete it;
- object to or restrict its use;
- withdraw your consent;
- receive it in a portable form;
- complain to a data protection authority.

Because we hold no account, we identify your data by the identifier on your device. Contact us and
we will tell you how to find it.
