Pencils Down: Exam Proctor

Support

Questions, a bug, a purchase that didn't arrive, or a data request: write to us.

support@pencilsdowngame.com

Send an email

It helps to include your phone model and the game version, shown in Settings › About.

Restore purchases

Item packs are linked to your Google Play or App Store account. After a reinstall or on a new device, open either:

  • Settings → Purchases → Restore purchases, or
  • Shop › Packs › Restore purchases.

Coins are not restored this way: they are stored on your device, not in an account. On Android, Google's Auto Backup (if you have it on) can bring the game's data, coins included, back on a new phone.

Turn off usage and crash data

Go to Settings → Privacy and terms → Usage and crash data and turn it off. This stops all collection at once and clears the analytics data stored on your device. See the Privacy Policy for what is collected.

Delete your data

To delete data the game has already sent, email support@pencilsdowngame.com. The game has no accounts, so its data is identified by an identifier on your device; we will reply with how to find it, then ask Google to delete the data. Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on the device.

Refunds

Payments are handled by the store, so refunds are requested there, under its refund policy:

  • Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google: support.google.com/googleplay

Game version

The version you have is shown in Settings › About.
